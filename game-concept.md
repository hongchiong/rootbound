# Game: Rootbound

## Description

Rootbound is a 2D pixel roguelike autobattler where you graft traits onto a single living seedling over the course of a run — watching it grow, mutate, and fight for you — until you reach the final boss with a creature that has never existed before and never will again.

## Core Fantasy

You are a gardener who builds living weapons.

Over the course of a run, you make a series of choices — grafting root, thorn, spore, bloom, and vine traits onto a single seedling — watching it visually transform with each decision. By the final boss, you've grown something that is entirely yours: a creature that reflects your strategy, your creativity, and your willingness to test combinations nobody expected.

The game never tells you the "right" build. It gives you ingredients and lets you discover what they become together.

## Unique Hook

Like Vampire Survivors, **AND ALSO** your progress is expressed through a single evolving sprite — your seedling grows, mutates, and visually encodes the decisions you made. At the end of every run, you have a unique creature to screenshot, name, and share. Upon completion of a run, your unique seedling and it's build should be easily sharable onto social media platforms.

The innovation is **personal investment in a single entity**, not a deck or a loadout. You watch your creation fight. Its survival _feels_ like your survival.
